The tale is told

Ah Bartleby! Ah humanity!

Some months after, a rumor reached the chambers. Bartleby, it was said, had been a subordinate clerk in the Dead Letter Office at Washington, and had been suddenly removed by a change in the administration. Letters sent in haste and hope, never reaching their mark, sorted for the flames.

“Dead letters! does it not sound like dead men?”
